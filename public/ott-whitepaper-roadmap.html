Whitepaper roadmap · working version

Learn. Verify. Build utility.

OTT Terminal develops in controlled phases. Learning and verifiable proof come first. Wallet actions, credentials, community governance and token concepts open only after technical, security and legal gates have been passed.

One function, one homeNo duplicate full pages. Every feature has one canonical route.
Proof before accessA click or signature alone never counts as validated completion.
Testnet before MainnetPayment, mint, transfer, voting and ownership are proven end to end first.
Phase 1

Terminal foundation

Stable public hubs, account-first onboarding, source identity, security boundaries and consistent OTT design.

  • Eight canonical hubs
  • EN/NL and Light/Dark
  • Evidence-based QA
Phase 2

Academy expansion

Practical XRPL learning routes with verified answer scoring, wallet safety and achievement credentials.

  • Courses and library
  • Wallet Academy
  • Verified NFT certificates
Phase 3

Web2 licence access

A normal software and invoice route for people and organisations that are not ready to use crypto.

  • Clear licence terms
  • Account entitlements
  • No forced crypto conversion
Phase 4

Marketplace and merchandise

OTT products, education bundles and holder utility with transparent fulfilment and support flows.

  • Product catalogue
  • Safe checkout
  • Transparent support totals
Phase 5

AI research assistant

Source-aware research for XRPL, ISO 20022, SWIFT, MiCAR, DeFi and tokenisation, with human review before publishing.

  • Source-backed research
  • Newsroom workflow
  • Human approval gate
Phase 6

XP, OTT token concepts and NFT utility

Verified XP may become eligible for defined OTT utility only after policy, security and legal review. NFT holders may qualify for transparent monthly utility distributions or campaigns. Governance can combine one-wallet proof with capped, auditable XP or utility-token weighting.

  • XP-to-utility conversion rules
  • Monthly NFT-holder utility campaigns
  • Capped voting leverage with public proof
Phase 6 is a design and review phase, not a promise of token value, yield, profit, guaranteed airdrops or automatic conversion. Final utility, eligibility, transferability and voting weight require separate technical testing, abuse controls and legal approval before public activation.
